• New: your squadmates' tasks are in your task list. Tasks only a friend has sit in the usual categories, after yours, with a crosshatched background: you can open, select and pin them, but not tick them. The panel header shows "+N from squad".
• New: every task row and popup shows a small colour box and the name of each friend who has that task (this replaces "Also: …"). Each friend's ☰ Tasks switch is now on by default; if you had turned it off for someone, it stays off.
• New: on the map, a squadmate's task you don't have looks like your own markers of that category (same shape, colour, item picture and badges), only see-through and under yours. Click it to select it. Small squares in each friend's colour sit under a task's main spot (up to four, then "+N"), replacing the dots at the bottom left.
• New: friends see each other's last position on the map: a small dot in the friend's colour with a heading arrow and their name. It is where they took their last screenshot in the raid (not live tracking), it shows only on the map you are on, fades after 5 minutes and is gone after 30. Each friend chip has a new ◎ Pos switch (on by default) and says "on Customs" while that friend is on another map.
• Changed: Share my tasks is now on by default, and there is a new Share my position next to it, also on by default. Updating switches both on once, so if you don't want to share your tasks or your position with your squad, turn them off in Settings → Squad. The app sends nothing while a switch is off, and nothing at all if you never join a squad.
• Removed: the AI Categorize chat box. Tasks still sort into the default categories by themselves (that never used AI), and Auto-sort, your own categories and moving tasks by hand work as before. The Reasoning setting is gone; your OpenAI key stays for Scan tasks and reading your extracts.
• Note: once, on the first start of 2.10.0, every task goes back to its default category. Categories you (or the AI) made are kept, now empty. Close any open Squad Task Map browser tabs before updating, so an old tab can't undo this.
• Changed: the default AI model is GPT-6 Luna (gpt-6-luna), used by Scan tasks and reading your extracts. If you never picked a model, the app switches you once on first start; a model you chose yourself is kept. If your OpenAI account can't use gpt-6-luna, pick another model in Settings → OpenAI.
